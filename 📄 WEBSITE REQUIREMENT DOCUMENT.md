 **WEBSITE REQUIREMENT DOCUMENT** 

**Project Name: R Education Tuition Website** 

**Phase 1 – Institutional Information Website** 

 **PROJECT OVERVIEW** 

**1.1 Objective** 

To develop a professional website for **R Education Tuition Institute** that provides  structured information about: 

• Institute details 

• Faculties 

• Students & results 

• Weekly timetable 

• Test schedule 

• Parent meetings 

• Extra-curricular activities 

• Contact information 

The purpose is to: 

• Increase admissions 

• Build trust with parents 

• Present a structured academic system 

• Showcase discipline & results 

 **PROJECT SCOPE (PHASE 1\)** 

This phase includes only informational website functionality. 

 No login system 

 No online payment 

 No student portal 

 No attendance dashboard 

These will be included in Phase 2\.  
 **TARGET USERS** 

1\. Parents (Primary audience) 

2\. Students 

3\. Prospective new admissions 

4\. Institute management 

 **WEBSITE STRUCTURE (SITEMAP)** The website should contain the following pages: 1\. Home 

2\. About Us 

3\. Our Faculties 

4\. Our Students 

5\. Timetable 

6\. Test Schedule 

7\. Parents Meeting 

8\. Extra-Curricular Activities 

9\. Testimonials 

10.Contact Us
